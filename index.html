About

01 · Welcome

CFK Productions

CFK is a lean, fast-paced film production company and trusted source for all things related to Asian cinema.

Established 2005 in Hong Kong CFK's purpose has always been to document and showcase the fascinating sub-culture of Asian film-making – via the highest quality re-issues, restorations, documentaries, audio commentaries and expanded stories.

02 · Our Network

Asia Based

Our production team covers the entire East Asian film industry, and is very much 'on-the-ground'.

Thanks to this presence, CFK has direct and often exclusive access the original film prints and media archives.

And most importantly of all the CFK team has personal access to the amazing people who created these films, the personalities who make Asian cinema so endearing.

03 · Connection

Right Here

Whatever project you have in mind – regional licensing, documentaries, archive restoration or research (or you just want to connect with fellow film buffs) – CFK is right here.

When all is said and done, CFK wants share our passion & enthusiasm for Asian film-making with fans worldwide.
